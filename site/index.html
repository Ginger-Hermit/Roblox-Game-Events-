How do I get a webhook?
  1. In Discord, open your server and long-press (or right-click) the channel you want.
  2. Tap Edit Channel, then Integrations, then Webhooks.
  3. Tap New Webhook, then Copy Webhook URL.
  4. Paste it above and tap Test. A test post should show in your channel.

You need Manage Webhooks permission on the server. Keep the link private: anyone with it can post in your channel.

3. How it auto posts

  1. Once a day, it checks your game for new events.
  2. Each new event posts to your channel with its times, type and a Play link.
  3. Tap the post to open the event on Roblox. Old events never repost.
