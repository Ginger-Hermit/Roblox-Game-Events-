Game events, straight to Discord

Pick any Roblox game. New events post to your Discord channel automatically.

3. How it auto posts

  1. Once a day, it checks your game for new events.
  2. Each new event posts to your channel with its start and end time.
  3. Tap the post to open the event on Roblox. Old events never repost.

To switch it on

  1. Tap Copy settings and paste them into events/settings.json on GitHub.
  2. Add your webhook as a GitHub secret called DISCORD_WEBHOOK.
