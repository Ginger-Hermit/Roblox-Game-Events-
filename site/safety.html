Stay safe
  • Your Discord webhook is like a password: anyone with it can post in your channel.
  • Never put it in a chat, post or screenshot. Save it only as a GitHub secret.
  • Only use the real link: roblox-game-events.pages.dev
  • We never ask for your Roblox or Discord password, email, name or payment.
Webhook leaked?
  • In Discord: Edit Channel, Integrations, Webhooks, then delete that webhook.
  • Make a new one and update the DISCORD_WEBHOOK secret on GitHub.
Privacy
  • When you tap Turn on, the game and webhook are saved on our helper (Cloudflare) so it can post daily. Turn off deletes them. Nothing else is kept, and there are no accounts.
  • Test and Post send one message straight from your browser to Discord. Nothing else is sent anywhere.
  • The daily check only reads the game's public event list from Roblox. It never logs in.
  • Saved on your device only: theme, text size, and games and webhooks you choose to remember. Delete them in Settings.
  • No ads and no tracking. The site host may keep normal visit records, like any website.
Real risks
  • Account ban: none. It never logs in to Roblox or Discord, so your accounts can't be banned for using it.
  • Webhook leak: low. Your webhook is stored on our helper. If it ever leaked, someone could spam that one channel. They can't read messages, see members or touch the rest of your server. Delete the webhook to stop it.
  • Spam from us: none. At most 3 posts a day per game, and old events never repost.
  • Wrong or missed posts: low. Roblox can change its event info at any time, so a post may be late, missing or out of date.
  • It stops working: possible. It runs on free Cloudflare and GitHub plans. If it breaks or shuts down, posts just stop. Nothing else is affected.
