Stay safe
  • Your Discord webhook is like a password: anyone with it can post in your channel.
  • Never put it in a chat, post or screenshot. Only paste it into this site.
  • Only use the real link: roblox-game-events.pages.dev
  • We never ask for your Roblox or Discord password, email, name or payment.
Webhook leaked?
  • In Discord: Edit Channel, Integrations, Webhooks, then delete that webhook.
  • Make a new one, tap Turn off with the old one, then Turn on with the new one.
Privacy
  • When you tap Turn on, the game and webhook are saved on our helper so it can post daily. Turn off deletes them. Nothing else is kept, and there are no accounts.
  • Test and Post send one message straight from your browser to Discord. Nothing else is sent anywhere.
  • The daily check only reads the game's public event list from Roblox. It never logs in.
  • Saved on your device only: theme, text size, and games and webhooks you choose to remember. Delete them in Settings.
  • No ads and no tracking. The site host may keep normal visit records, like any website.
Real risks
Account ban: very unlikelyIt never logs in to Roblox or Discord for you.
Webhook leak: lowSomeone could spam that one channel. Delete the webhook to stop it.
Spam from us: none3 posts a day at most. Old events never repost.
Wrong posts: lowRoblox can change event info at any time.
Stops working: possibleIt runs on free hosting.
