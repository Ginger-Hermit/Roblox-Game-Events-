# Privacy and safety

![Help](https://img.shields.io/badge/section-Help-orange)

</div>

**Saved on the helper (after Start):** game number, game name, webhook (locked), list of seen events, the date you turned it on.

**Saved on your phone:** your theme and text size, and webhooks you chose to remember. Settings → **Forget all** clears them.

**Not saved:** anything else. No accounts, no tracking.

> [!WARNING]
> **Risk:** anyone with your webhook link can post in your channel. Don't share it. If it leaks, delete it in Discord and make a new one.

> [!NOTE]
> Stop deletes everything saved about that game and webhook.

Full details: [Stay safe](https://roblox-game-events.pages.dev/safety.html).
