# Limits

![Learn](https://img.shields.io/badge/section-Learn-blue)

</div>

| What | Limit |
|---|---|
| Checks | Once a day |
| Posts per game per day | 3 |
| Games per channel | 5 |
| Games on the helper in total | 300 |
| Post again | Once every 10 minutes |
| Events checked per game | Latest 25 |

When the helper is full, Turn on says "It's full right now".
