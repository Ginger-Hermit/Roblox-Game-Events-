# Glossary

![Help](https://img.shields.io/badge/section-Help-orange)

</div>

- **Webhook**: a secret Discord link that lets an app post in one channel.
- **Helper**: the small Cloudflare service that saves what you turned on and does the posting.
- **Event**: a Roblox in-game event (shown under "Events" on a game's page).
- **Game number**: the digits in a game's link, e.g. `17450551531`.
